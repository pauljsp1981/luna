LUNA

LUNA is a private, personal-use assistant application that runs on its owner's own computer.

LUNA connects to the owner's own Gmail account, with the owner's permission through Google sign-in, so the owner can ask LUNA to read and send the owner's own email from the owner's own machine.

LUNA is not a public service. It has no other users, no accounts for anyone else, and no server. Everything runs locally on the owner's computer, for the owner only.
