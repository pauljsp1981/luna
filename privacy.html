LUNA Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

LUNA is a private, personal-use application that runs entirely on its owner's own computer. This policy explains what LUNA does with data.

What LUNA accesses

With the owner's explicit permission, granted through Google's sign-in and consent screen, LUNA accesses the owner's own Gmail account so the owner can ask LUNA to read and send the owner's own email.

What LUNA does not do

LUNA does not collect data for anyone else. It has no server, no other users, and no advertising. It does not sell, rent, share, or transfer any data to any third party. Email data stays on the owner's own computer.

Data storage and deletion

Any email data LUNA reads is processed locally on the owner's machine. The owner can revoke LUNA's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page, and can delete the application's files and credentials from their computer at any time.
